The Silicon Ring (SiRi) detector is a $E - \Delta E$- detector system. It is composed of 6 detector segments, with two detector layers in each segment. The system works by measuring charges collected from an interaction. A calibration with a known source is then used to determine amount of collected charges proportional to the energy of a particle. 

The $\Delta E$-layer is the thinnest layer.
